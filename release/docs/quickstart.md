# Quickstart Guide

## Getting Started with GreenChainz

### 1. Login

Open the GreenChainz panel in Revit or Forma and authenticate with your account credentials.

**Demo Access:**

### 2. Select a Material

Navigate to any building element or material in your project. The GreenChainz panel automatically shows sustainability data for the selected material category.

### 3. View Sustainability Data

For each material, you'll see:
- **EPD Summary** — Environmental Product Declaration data
- **Embodied Carbon** — kg CO2e per unit
- **Lifecycle Impact** — cradle-to-gate environmental metrics
- **Certifications** — third-party verifications

### 4. Compare Alternatives

Click "Compare" to see alternative materials with:
- Lower carbon footprint options
- Cost vs. sustainability tradeoffs
- Regional supplier availability
- Certification status

### 5. Discover Suppliers

Browse verified suppliers who provide the selected material:
- Filter by region, certification, lead time
- View supplier sustainability profiles
- See pricing and availability

### 6. Send an RFQ

When you've identified the right material and supplier:
1. Click "Request Quote"
2. Confirm project details and quantities
3. Submit — the RFQ goes directly to the supplier
4. Track status in the GreenChainz dashboard

---

## Tips

- Use the **carbon comparison** view to justify material swaps to stakeholders
- **Favorite** frequently used sustainable materials for quick access
- **Export** sustainability reports for LEED/BREEAM documentation
