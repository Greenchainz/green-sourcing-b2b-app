> Distilled from "Sustainable Material Supplier Ecosystem Analysis" (2026).
> Cross-references: [`HUNTER_AGENTS_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../HUNTER_AGENTS_ARCHITECTURE.md),

> Typed module: [`lib/research/supplierCategories.ts`](../../lib/research/supplierCategories.ts).

**Core premise:** the sustainable-material suppliers we need are real,
technically superior, and **invisible to the Tier 1 architecture firms and
asset managers who are legally and ethically mandated to find them.**
GreenChainz exists to close the "friction of discovery" gap. We are not
selling sustainability to suppliers — they are already converted.
**We are selling survival through visibility.**

## Readiness Scoring Rubric

Use this to score scraped companies before placing them in a tier.

| Signal | +High | +Med | +Low |
|--------|-------|------|------|
| EPD published (EC3, EPD International) | Yes | Claimed, not verified | None |
| ASTM / ISO test data | Public | Partial | None |
| LEED credit mapping on site | Yes | Generic mention | None |
| Scaled manufacturing (>1M units/yr or >$10M rev) | Yes | 100k–1M or $1–10M | <100k or pre-revenue |
| Tier 1 architect case studies | ≥3 | 1–2 | 0 |
| Years in market | ≥10 | 3–9 | <3 |

A company scoring mostly "High" → Founding 50 outreach priority.
A company scoring mostly "Low" → Trojan Horse EPD-assistance outreach.

## How To Use This File

- **Scrapers** must resolve every discovered company to a `SupplierCategory`
  and a `MaterialReadiness` value (see typed module).
- **Outreach templates** must start from the pitch angle listed for that
  category/company; do not write generic "we help sustainable suppliers"
  copy.
- **Marketplace search** must allow filtering by both category and readiness
  so architects looking for "production-ready" can skip the startups and
  architects looking for "novel" can find them.
- **SWAP Engine** should weight alternate suggestions by both carbon delta
  AND readiness — suggesting a Low-readiness product to a risk-averse
  Spec Writer wastes an Interceptor impression.
