# AECO Technology Partner Program – GreenChainz Positioning

## Value Proposition

GreenChainz enhances Autodesk workflows by introducing a procurement-native sustainability layer that connects design decisions directly to real-world material sourcing.

Unlike static analysis tools, GreenChainz enables:
- **Direct supplier engagement** – Designers connect with verified material suppliers without leaving their tools
- **Real-time procurement workflows** – RFQs initiated from within Revit/Forma based on actual project specs
- **Verified sustainability data** – EPDs and carbon data tied to real products from real suppliers

## Technical Integration

- **Autodesk integration endpoints** – Hosted within the main GreenChainz platform (`green-sourcing-b2b-app`)
- **Revit Plugin** – Native ribbon panel for material sustainability data
- **Forma Extension** – Early-stage design sustainability insights
- **Backend** – Azure-hosted API, PostgreSQL, secure OAuth

---

## Market Opportunity

- $2.2T global construction market increasingly mandated to report embodied carbon
- EU taxonomy, LEED v5, and SEC climate rules driving demand
- No current marketplace app bridges sustainability data + procurement in one workflow
- GreenChainz positions Autodesk as the platform where sustainable procurement happens
