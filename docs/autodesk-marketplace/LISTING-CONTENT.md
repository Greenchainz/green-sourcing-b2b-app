## Short Description

> Embed real-time sustainable material data, embodied carbon insights, and supplier procurement workflows directly into Autodesk Revit and Forma.

---

## Full Description

GreenChainz integrates directly into Autodesk Revit and Autodesk Forma to make sustainability actionable at the moment design and procurement decisions are made.

The platform connects building designers, contractors, and suppliers through real-time access to:
- Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs)
- Embodied carbon data
- Verified sustainable product catalogs
- Supplier sourcing workflows
- RFQ and procurement pipelines

Inside Revit and Forma, users can evaluate material choices not just by cost and performance, but by measurable environmental impact.

GreenChainz bridges the gap between design intent and real-world procurement by enabling:
- Data-driven sustainable material selection
- Direct engagement with verified suppliers
- Streamlined RFQ workflows tied to actual project needs
- Transparency into lifecycle and carbon impact data

This transforms sustainability from a reporting exercise into a real-time decision-making advantage within Autodesk workflows.

## Key Features

1. **Real-time EPD data integration** – Access verified Environmental Product Declarations within your design tool
2. **Embodied carbon insights** – See carbon impact at the material level before specifying
3. **Supplier discovery and filtering** – Find verified sustainable material suppliers
4. **RFQ workflow from design environment** – Initiate procurement without leaving Revit/Forma
5. **Sustainable product catalog** – Browse alternatives with environmental performance data
6. **Design-to-procurement continuity** – Seamless workflow from material selection to sourcing

## Installation Instructions

### Revit Plugin

1. Download the GreenChainz installer
2. Run installer (requires admin)
3. Launch Revit → GreenChainz panel appears in ribbon
4. Authenticate with GreenChainz account

### Forma Extension

1. Open Autodesk Forma
2. Navigate to Extensions panel
3. Search "GreenChainz" or enable via URL
4. Authenticate with GreenChainz account

## Security & Trust

- Secure user authentication (OAuth 2.0)
- Role-based access control (RBAC)
- No storage of Autodesk credentials
- Data encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+)
