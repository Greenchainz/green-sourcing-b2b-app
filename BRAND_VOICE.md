## The Archetype: Principled Rebel

GreenChainz is the gnat. The industry is the giant.

The gnat doesn't win by being bigger. It wins by being faster, more precise, and impossible to fully ignore. We break rules because the rules are broken — not because we don't care. Every claim we make is backed by an EPD, a certification, or verified data. The attitude is earned.

**We are:**
- Confident without being arrogant
- Sharp without being aggressive
- Credentialed without being corporate
- Fun without sacrificing trust

**We are not:**
- Self-righteous ("saving the planet, you're welcome")
- Startup-cringe ("disrupting the paradigm of sustainable synergies")
- Aggressive toward competitors by name
- Sloppy — the gnat is precise

## Voice Pillars

### 1. Confident, Not Corporate
Big competitors write in press release voice. We write like a person who knows something they don't.

- ❌ *"GreenChainz provides a comprehensive verified sustainable material procurement solution for AEC professionals."*
- ✅ *"Find certified green materials in minutes. No spreadsheets. No greenwashing. No excuses."*

### 2. Sharp, Not Aggressive
The chase is fun, the underdog wins, but nobody gets hurt. We're not bitter. We're better.

- ❌ *"Unlike legacy platforms, GreenChainz..."*
- ✅ *"The industry spent 20 years faxing sustainability data. We built the fix."*

### 3. Moral Edge, Not Moralizing
We care about the planet. We don't lecture about it. The credential does the talking — we just make it easy to use.

- ❌ *"Sustainability is everyone's responsibility."*
- ✅ *"Your project gets LEED credits. The planet gets a break. You get home on time."*

### 4. Irreverent but Trustworthy
The joke lands because the data backs it up. Never sacrifice credibility for a quip.

- ❌ *"Big material companies hate this one weird trick."*
- ✅ *"EPD-verified. LEED-aligned. Way less boring than your current process."*

## Legal / Compliance Voice Rules

- **Never use "verified" as a standalone brand claim** without specifying the standard (EPD-verified, LEED-aligned, third-party audited). FTC Green Guides apply.
- **Never claim certification** for materials not yet processed through the platform's audit pipeline.
- **Partner logos** (Autodesk, Microsoft) follow their respective brand guidelines — do not alter, recolor, or place on unapproved backgrounds.

## The Mascot: G — The Badass Gnat

### Concept
G is the GreenChainz mascot. Small, dangerous, and holding receipts. G is the embodiment of the brand — technically precise, morally grounded, and absolutely unbothered by being underestimated.

### Personality
- **Confident** — G has done the math. G is right.
- **Principled** — G cares about outcomes, not optics. The EPD matters more than the press release.
- **Dry humor** — G doesn't need to explain the joke. If you got it, you got it.
- **Never petty** — G doesn't punch down or trash competitors by name. G just wins.

### Visual Direction
- Dark silhouette, geometric/angular lines — built like it means business
- Hard hat optional; rolled EPD document or clipboard with a checkmark as signature accessory
- Colorway: GreenChainz green on black — high contrast, no softness
- Style influence: military unit patch meets skate brand logo
- NOT a cute cartoon bug — G is intimidating at thumbnail size
- NOT 3D rendered — bold flat vector only

### What G Says
- *"EPD in hand. Clock running. Let's go."*
- *"You could keep doing it the old way. G doesn't recommend it."*
- *"Certified. Scored. Already in your inbox."*
- *"The giant didn't see G coming. They never do."*

### What G Never Says
- Anything preachy about the environment
- Anything that can't be backed by platform data
- Anything mean about a named competitor
