Getting Started

🔌

Revit Plugin

Install the GreenChainz plugin for Autodesk Revit 2024–2025. Access embodied carbon data and supplier material specs directly inside your Revit project.

  • Download from the Autodesk Design & Make Marketplace
  • Sign in with your GreenChainz account
  • Open the GreenChainz panel from the Add-Ins tab
🏗️

Autodesk Forma Extension

Use the GreenChainz extension for Autodesk Forma to surface verified sustainable material options during early-stage design.

  • Install from the Forma Extension Library
  • Authenticate via your GreenChainz account
  • Browse and compare material carbon data in context
📦

Supplier Onboarding

Register your company as a verified sustainable materials supplier. Upload EPD certificates, product data sheets, and connect your catalog.

  • Create a supplier account at greenchainz.com
  • Complete the verification workflow
  • Publish your product listings to the marketplace

Platform Overview

🌿

Embodied Carbon Data

GreenChainz sources embodied carbon data from EPD International and Building Transparency (EC3) to provide verified, comparable A1–A3 carbon figures for building materials.

🔒

Security & Privacy

Your data is protected by Azure-hosted infrastructure with Microsoft Entra ID authentication. Review our security overview and privacy policy.
